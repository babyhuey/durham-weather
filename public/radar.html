How the radar forecast has done

The forecast frames slide the newest radar image along the direction the rain moved over the last 15 minutes, the same basic method as the open-source pySTEPS library. It is good at timing rain that already exists and blind to storms that haven't formed yet. Each forecast is checked against the radar once its time arrives; scores are kept on this device only.
